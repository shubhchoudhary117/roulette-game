How to play? Provably fair settings Game rules My bet history

European roulette — 37 pockets (0–36). Every round gives you 15 seconds to place chips before the wheel spins.

  1. Pick a chip value at the bottom.
  2. Click a number or an outside area to drop a chip — click again to stack.
  3. Press Place bet to lock in (bets lock automatically when the timer ends).
  4. The pointer decides the winning number and payouts settle instantly.
Bet Covers Payout
Straight number 1 number 35 : 1
Column (2:1) 12 numbers 2 : 1
Dozen 12 numbers 2 : 1
Red / Black 18 numbers 1 : 1
Even / Odd 18 numbers 1 : 1
1–18 / 19–36 18 numbers 1 : 1

Zero loses every outside bet — house edge 2.70%.

Each round is generated from a server seed and your client seed. The hash is published before the spin so results can be verified afterwards.
